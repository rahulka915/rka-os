# RKA OS River Stone Material Specification

## Objective

Create the visual impression of a thin physical surface carved from polished graphite river stone.

The result should feel:

- matte
- calm
- tactile
- premium
- symmetrical
- clearly recognisable as a UI component

The result should not feel:

- metallic
- glossy
- glassy
- plastic
- liquid
- melted
- irregular
- decorative

## Lighting direction

Primary light comes from the upper-left.

The light should be broad and diffuse.

The user should perceive curvature without seeing an obvious gradient boundary.

## Central face

The middle 70–80% of the surface should remain close to one base tone.

Avoid visible segmentation into:

- light upper half
- dark lower half

## Upper ambient light

Use broad overlapping fields.

The upper ambient light should:

- begin above the surface
- fade before the middle
- be stronger near the upper-left
- avoid forming a straight line

## Edge catch

The polished edge catch should:

- be extremely subtle
- appear only near parts of the upper-left edge
- remain discontinuous
- never become a complete border
- not increase mechanically with elevation

## Lower occlusion

The lower region should be slightly darker to imply physical thickness.

Lower occlusion should affect:

- lower edge
- lower-left corner
- lower-right corner

It should never outline:

- the top
- the full sides
- the complete perimeter

## Shadows

Use two distinct shadows.

### Contact shadow

A short, dark shadow immediately beneath the object.

Purpose:

- ground the surface
- communicate contact
- prevent floating

### Ambient shadow

A larger, softer shadow surrounding the contact shadow.

Purpose:

- communicate elevation
- separate the surface from the background

## Depth hierarchy

From shallowest to deepest:

1. Chip
2. Header
3. List row
4. Card
5. Hero
6. Tray

Depth should change through:

- contact shadow
- ambient shadow
- lower-edge weight
- upper light strength
- radius

Not through a brighter silver border.

## Geometry

River Stone surfaces remain clearly recognisable as normal UI components.

Use:

- symmetry
- large smooth corner transitions
- slightly flatter upper edges
- subtle lower-body fullness
- intentional proportions

Avoid:

- random distortion
- excessive asymmetry
- organic blobs
- melted contours
